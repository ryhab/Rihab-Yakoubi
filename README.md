## 👋 About me

I'm a DevOps and Systems Engineer based in Berlin with 8+ years of experience running the IT and cloud foundations that teams depend on every day. My work sits where identity, endpoints, cloud and automation meet: getting the right people the right access, keeping fleets secure and compliant, and making infrastructure cost what it should.

I care about systems that are secure by default, automated end to end, and easy for the next engineer to understand.

- 🔐 **Identity & access** — Okta, SSO, SCIM provisioning, RBAC and MFA rollouts
- 💻 **Endpoint management** — Jamf, Intune and zero-touch device onboarding
- ☁️ **Cloud & FinOps** — AWS and GCP, cost optimisation, licence and vendor management
- ⚙️ **Automation** — Terraform, CI/CD, Python and Bash to remove manual work
- 🤖 **Currently exploring** — AI agents and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for IT operations
